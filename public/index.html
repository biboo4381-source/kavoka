01 · The product

A premium gift that tells a story, at a price people will try.

The problem

Coffee lovers want a better chocolate gift

  • Most of Singapore drinks coffee every day, but most chocolate gifts don't taste of coffee or say anything about where they come from.
  • Local craft coffee bars cost S$13–15 for 50 g, which is too much for a first try.
  • Supermarket chocolate is cheap but doesn't feel special enough to give.
Our answer

KAVOKA coffee-infused chocolate

  • Indonesian coffee is steeped in cocoa butter and blended into the chocolate itself. It isn't a filling.
  • Four strengths, so there's a bar for every coffee drinker, from a latte fan to a double-espresso fan.
  • Espresso-and-gold packaging with a modern batik pattern, ready to give straight off the shelf.

Dual Indonesian origin

Indonesian cacao (Schoko couverture) and Indonesian coffee in one bar. "Made in Indonesia" is part of the brand, not just a line on the label.

Gift-ready packaging

Espresso-and-gold design with a batik motif. Comes as a single bar or a 5-bar Hard Box for festive and corporate gifts.

Under S$10

S$9.90 against Fossa's S$13–15. It's an affordable premium chocolate, so trying it for the first time is easy.

Target customer

Urban coffee-loving adults aged 25–49 with above-median income, working in the CBD or living in central private housing, who buy for themselves and as gifts (festive and corporate). Secondary: tourists and expatriates.

02 · The brand

Two origins, one story.

KA(kao) + KO(pi) = KAVOKA

The name joins Indonesian kakao (cacao) and kopi (coffee): cacao for richness, coffee for character, and Indonesia as the origin. The emblem puts a cacao pod and a coffee bean inside the letter K.

Packaging

Ready to give, straight off the shelf.

A sleeve with a modern batik pattern, a premium foil inner wrap and a gift box. The label follows Singapore's English-label rules: ingredients, allergens, nutrition, origin, importer, lot number and best-before date.

How it's made

Cocoa-butter infusion.

Indonesian coffee, fine cacao and a careful process. The coffee goes into the chocolate itself, which keeps it a solid bar (HS 1806.32) rather than a filled one.

Steep

Steep ground Indonesian coffee in melted cocoa butter.

Strain

Strain out the grounds and keep the aromatic infused butter.

Blend

Blend the infusion into dark chocolate couverture.

Temper

Control the temperature for a glossy finish and a clean snap.

Mould & pack

Mould the bars, wrap them in foil and sleeve them for export.
